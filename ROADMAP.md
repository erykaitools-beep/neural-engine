# Roadmap: questions, not promises

1. **Independent reproduction.** First have an outside group verify the archived scores, then reproduce paired runs from the published fixtures. Report nonmatches and platform details; do not weaken a comparison to obtain a pass.
2. **When can the network safely decide?** Compare evidence accumulated until a physically distinguishing sample with a matched-latency control. Test on unseen rhythms sharing longer prefixes. Report premature emissions, missed GO signals and latency, not just a mean score.
3. **Memory under competing feedback.** Separate two genuinely different operators across additional cores, contact strengths and noise sequences. Include two consecutive, consistently wrong confirmations and measure both protection and adaptation delay.
4. **Functional geometry.** Permute the mapping between positions and a fixed graph while matching degrees, connectivity and input fields. Use a task away from ceiling. Test distance-dependent delay only in a separate preregistered comparison.
5. **Scaling and energy.** Establish matched-quality baselines, report resource cost and independent energy measurements before comparing efficiencies. General sequences and language are long-term goals, not demonstrated capabilities.

These items are proposals; none authorizes changing a historical result or reclassifying a failed criterion.
